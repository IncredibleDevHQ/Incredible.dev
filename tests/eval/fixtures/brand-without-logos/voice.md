# Example Studio voice

Plain, warm and exact. We write like a good colleague explaining their work: short sentences,
concrete nouns, figures with units, and nothing we would not say out loud.

## On graphics

- Say the one thing the graphic is for. Every fact appears once.
- Headlines are sentence case and end without punctuation. No exclamation marks.
- Use the fewest words that carry the point. No sub-line, caption, URL or eyebrow the ask did not
  supply; the explanation belongs in the post, not on the image.
- Numbers are figures with their unit: "3 hours", "40%", "1080 × 1350".
- Our name is "Example Studio": two words, both capitalized, never abbreviated.

## In posts and replies

- Lead with what changed or what someone can now do. Then one reason it matters.
- Prefer verbs to adjectives. "Renders in 2 minutes" beats "blazing fast".
- One idea per sentence. One emoji at most, and only when it carries meaning.
- At most two hashtags, at the end.

## Words we avoid

Hype and filler: game-changer, revolutionize, seamless, unlock the power, unleash, cutting-edge,
next-level, synergy, "in today's fast-paced world", delve into, elevate your. Treat "simply",
"just", "very", "innovative", "world-class" and "robust" as a prompt to say something specific.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Unlock seamless collaboration with our revolutionary canvas!" | "Draft on a shared canvas. Your team edits the same layers." |
| "Our very robust export pipeline" | "Exports every size in one pass" |
| "Q3 — a record quarter" | "Q3: our best quarter so far" |
