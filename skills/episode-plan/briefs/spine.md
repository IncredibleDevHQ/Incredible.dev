# The spine

The brief is signed off: the idea, who it is for, the length, and the ledger of what the film
may state. You pick the one thing the viewer follows from the first frame to the last.
Everything else is written from it, by people who will not see the material the way you do, so
be exact.

## Read

- `brief.json` and `claims.json` in the plan folder. They stand as the team signed them off:
  change neither, and report the brief back word for word.
- The material the brief's `source` row names: the post, the docs, the notes.
- [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for what an episode is, and [../references/sections.md](../references/sections.md)
  for the form of what you write.

## The spine

The spine is one thing the viewer follows from the first frame to the last. In a technical
explainer it is nearly always a worked example: one real input, carried through every stage,
changing form at each. With a spine, every beat has the same thing to point at. Without one, a
five-minute film is five one-minute films.

Write three candidates of different kinds, then pick one:

- a worked example followed through (one query, one request, one packet, one commit);
- one diagram that grows (a map, a pipeline, a table that fills);
- a contrast held side by side (the old way and the new, the naive version and the real one).

For each, say the thing itself in one sentence, with the real example in it, and in `detail` say
honestly, in two or three sentences, which chapters it would carry and which it would not. Pick
the one every stage of the explanation can do something to. Where a second candidate suits part
of the film, say so in the pick's `detail`: it can be the set for those chapters. The `detail`
is why this spine, not a plan of the film: the chapter list comes next, from someone else.

## Bar

- The spine stands alone: someone who reads only that sentence knows what they will watch.
- The example is real and specific: an actual query, file name, number, taken from the ledger.
  Where the ledger marks its details illustrative, use the names it gives and add none.
- No packaging: nothing about the channel, the brand or the viewer subscribing.

## Report

The spine you picked and why, in a line or two. Then the JSON your brief asks for, whole.
