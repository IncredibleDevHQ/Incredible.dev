# Case studies

**Pending: the sample-brand case study.** It will be a launch film the agent makes for the sample
brand, Example Studio (`examples/brands/example-studio/`), with a real model, during the evaluation
slice. Nothing here is invented ahead of that run.

When it exists, it will contain:

- `README.md`: the brief as sent, the phases with their estimated and measured times, the number of
  helpers and their efforts, the approvals and what each changed, and the lessons, each tied to a
  timestamp, a file or a measurement from the run;
- `briefs-as-sent.md`: the filled concept, act-builder, critic, helper and sound briefs, as the
  helpers received them;
- `SHOTLIST.md` and the approved board sheets (the brand's own work, so they may be committed);
- the project's `timeline.js`, `STYLE.md` and `API.md`, and each act's limits log;
- the critics' reviews at every gate, and the measured results: per-act review scores, the motion
  curve against the animatic, `video-score` findings, loudness.

Until then, the rules in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) and [orchestration.md](../../orchestration.md)
are defaults to weigh, each with the failure it prevents. Once the case study exists, re-check each
estimate in the sizing table against its measured times.
